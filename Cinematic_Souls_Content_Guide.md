## 🎯 CONTENT PILLARS (What We Make)

### 1. 🌌 AI Cinematic Short Films
Short narrative films (1–5 min) made entirely with AI-generated visuals.
- Each video tells a complete story — beginning, middle, end
- Stories should evoke emotion: wonder, loss, hope, mystery
- Examples: A lone astronaut discovers a dying planet | A warrior walks through a forgotten kingdom

### 2. 🎭 Emotional Storytelling Reels (Shorts)
60-second YouTube Shorts with powerful one-liners or quotes over cinematic AI visuals.
- Use voiceover or text on screen
- Hook in first 2 seconds
- End with a feeling — not just an image
- Examples: "What if machines could dream?" | "Every soul has a story the world never heard."

### 3. 🌍 Cinematic World-Building Showcases
Showcase entire AI-generated worlds, civilizations, or environments with ambient music.
- No dialogue needed — pure visual experience
- Think: Ancient lost cities, futuristic empires, alien oceans, post-apocalyptic Earth
- Voiceover optional — lore-style narration works great

### 4. 🤖 AI vs Imagination Series
Concept videos exploring "What if AI imagined [X]?"
- "What if AI imagined Heaven?" 
- "What if AI imagined the End of the Universe?"
- "What if AI imagined a World Without Humans?"
- High shareability, curiosity-driven titles

### 5. 🎵 Cinematic Mood Videos (Loopable / Ambient)
Longer videos (10–30 min) with looping cinematic AI visuals + music
- Great for passive views and watch time
- Themes: Study music + space visuals | Sleep music + underwater worlds | Focus music + ancient temples

## ✍️ SCRIPT RULES (Follow Every Time)

### Structure of Every Script:
```
[HOOK]        → First 3–5 seconds. A question, mystery, or bold statement.
[ATMOSPHERE]  → Set the world. Describe the visuals and emotion.
[STORY/CORE]  → The main journey, conflict, or exploration.
[CLIMAX]      → The emotional peak or visual payoff.
[ENDING]      → A feeling. A quote. A question. Leave them thinking.
[CTA]         → "Subscribe for more cinematic worlds" or similar.
```

### Voice & Tone Rules:
- ✅ Speak like a narrator of an epic film — calm, deep, powerful
- ✅ Use poetic language — metaphors, imagery, rhythm
- ✅ Keep sentences short and punchy for impact
- ❌ No casual YouTube language ("Hey guys", "smash that like button")
- ❌ No overly technical AI jargon
- ❌ No comedy or meme-style content — we are serious and artistic

### Keywords to Use in Scripts:
soul, forgotten, ancient, beyond, dream, silence, echo, infinite, void, born, light, shadow, destiny, awaken, cosmos, eternal, lost, unseen, rise

## 📹 VIDEO PRODUCTION GUIDE

### Visual Style:
- Color Grade: Dark, moody — deep blues, burning oranges, gold, crimson
- Lighting: Dramatic — backlit silhouettes, god rays, neon in darkness
- Camera Movement: Slow push-ins, sweeping pans, slow-motion reveals
- NO fast cuts — every transition should feel intentional

### Music:
- Genre: Cinematic orchestral, ambient electronic, dark atmospheric
- References: Hans Zimmer, Two Steps from Hell, Audiomachine, really slow motion
- Music must match the emotional arc of the script

### Voiceover Style:
- Deep, slow, deliberate
- Slight reverb/echo effect in post
- Pause between key lines for impact
