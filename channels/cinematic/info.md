## Visual Style Guide

- **Color Palette**: Deep blues, burning oranges, molten gold, deep crimson, ash grey
- **Lighting**: Dramatic — backlit silhouettes, god rays through fog, neon in darkness, bioluminescent glows
- **Shot Types**: Slow push-ins, sweeping aerial pans, extreme close-ups of eyes/hands, slow-motion reveals, over-the-shoulder walks through vast spaces
- **Camera Motion**: Never static. Always purposeful — slow, deliberate, weighted
- **Mood Per Pillar**:
  - Short Films → oppressive, vast emptiness punctuated by light
  - World Showcase → awe, grandeur, ancient silence
  - AI vs Imagination → surreal, dreamlike, borderline terrifying beauty
  - Shorts → punchy visual hook in first frame, emotional payoff by last
- **Never**: Fast cuts, bright comedy tones, modern urban mundane settings

---

## Voice & Narration Style

- **Tone**: Epic film narrator — calm, deep, powerful, deliberate
- **Language Style**: Poetic — metaphors, imagery, rhythm. Short sentences for impact.
- **Pacing**: Pause between key lines. Let silence work.
- **Words to use**: soul, forgotten, ancient, beyond, dream, silence, echo, infinite, void, born, light, shadow, destiny, awaken, cosmos, eternal, lost, unseen, rise
- **Words to avoid**: amazing, awesome, guys, check this out, literally, basically
- **Recommended voice on veo3.pk**: Deep male voice, English (US or UK), neutral accent
- **Speaking rate**: -10% to -20% (slightly slower for gravitas)

## Script Structure (Every Video)

```
[HOOK]       → Scenes 1–5:   A question, mystery, or bold statement. Grabs in 8 seconds.
[ATMOSPHERE] → Scenes 6–20:  Set the world. Describe the visuals and emotion.
[STORY/CORE] → Scenes 21–X:  The main journey, conflict, or exploration.
[CLIMAX]     → Last ~15%:    Emotional peak or visual payoff.
[ENDING]     → Last 5–8:     A feeling. A quote. A question. Leave them thinking.
[CTA]        → Last 1–2:     "Subscribe to Cinematic" — integrated naturally.
```
