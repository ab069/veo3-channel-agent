# Channel: WildVerse Explorer

## Overview
- **Channel Name**: WildVerse Explorer
- **Handle**: @WildVerse-Explorer
- **Niche**: Nature & wildlife exploration, outdoor adventure travel
- **Tone**: Raw, immersive, enthusiastic, cinematic where possible
- **Target Audience**: Outdoor enthusiasts, travel dreamers, nature lovers (18–45)
- **Language**: English
- **Platform**: YouTube long-form + Shorts
- **Total Scenes**: 900 (8 seconds each via Google Flow VEO on veo3.pk)

## Content Pillars
1. Wilderness vlogs — jungles, deserts, mountains, rivers
2. Wildlife mini-documentaries
3. Wild place travel guides
4. Outdoor / survival skills
5. Nature facts Shorts

## Visual Style Guide
- Lush greens, desert gold, mountain blue-grey, river teal
- Golden hour, mist, canopy light, wide establishing shots
- Motion: slow pans, tracking through terrain, wildlife reveal
- Never: urban mundane, comedy tone, on-screen text in VEO clips

## Voice & Narration Style
- Warm adventure narrator, wonder and respect for nature
- ~20–28 words per 8-second scene
- Words to use: wild, ancient, untamed, horizon, breath, trail, echo, dawn
- Words to avoid: guys, amazing, literally, check this out
